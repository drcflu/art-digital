MAIDAN

"Maidan" is a Cossack song-and-dance group formed in 1992 by the honored artist V.Drachev. The name of the group gets its origin from the place, where all Cossacks used to gather in the past and where important political decisions were made.. The group skillfully reproduces on stage Cossack songs and dances in their original form.

｢廣場｣是一個哥薩克的歌舞團，1992年由著名的藝術家卓奇耶夫所創，團名源自尼古拉宮殿(註:舞團的表演地點)，過去所有的哥薩克人曾經聚集於此，以進行住要的政治性決定，舞團巧妙地以原有的形式再現了哥薩克的歌舞於舞台上。

The Stars of St.Petersburg

"The Stars of St.Petersburg" is a dance group. The dancers are graduated from leading schools of choreography in Russia. Its repertoire covers lyrical dances of the Russian North, fiering dances of the Cossacks from the Don and Voronezh, dancing miniatures of the Urals and Siberia with their subtle humor. Spectators of all ages and tastes will be able to appreciate not only the ability of the performers to bring Russian national color to the stage, but also their highest skills, complexity of acrobatic feats and breath-taking leaps.

｢聖彼得之星｣是一個舞團，舞者都畢業於俄羅斯著名的舞蹈學校，它的舞碼涵蓋了俄羅斯北方熱情奔放的舞蹈、及來自沃羅涅什(俄羅斯西南部城市，近頓河Don and Voronezh）的激烈的哥薩克舞，舞蹈帶有微妙的幽默，是烏拉山脈與西伯利亞的縮影。這使得任何年紀與品味的觀眾都能欣賞，不僅欣賞表演者把俄羅斯民族色彩帶到舞台，而且也能欣賞到他們高超的技術、特技的業績與驚人的跳躍。
