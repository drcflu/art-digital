The dépaysement means to remove an image from its original context and to juxtapose upon completely different contexts. This term is originated from the famous phrase of Lautréamont: “As beautiful as the chance encounter of a sewing machine and an umbrella on an operating table.” The juxtaposed images are removed from their proper contexts, including their form, texture, scale, space, color, light, statement as the  Surrealist R. Magritte did. But we try to add the element of movement, so the still images begin to move......
