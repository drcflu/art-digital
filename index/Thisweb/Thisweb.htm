關於本站與站主∕About the web & webmaster

       本站確是一 個小人物對大環境的無限關懷，無論是有形的城市或鄉村，版主會欣賞其間的美，針貶其中的醜；也不論是無形的文化或教育，更要張揚其間 的善，追蹤其中的惡，因為只有文化教育的善，才能帶來環境的美。而當欣賞到城鄉之美時，站主 會用GIF動畫將之表現出來，因為GIF動畫會將城鄉之美的韻律顯現出來，它可以說是一種motion painting，讓我們仔細端詳人世間或大自然的韻律，這是傳統繪畫或攝影作不到的部分。

        至於醜惡的部分只好「也管他人瓦上霜」，就像好心的撒瑪利亞人一樣，不能見死不救，必須見義勇為，多加關懷與批評，務期大環境有朝一 日能止於至善至美。

       本站架設已經歷時十四年，但因窮忙，疏於耕耘，目 前的改版也算是個紀念。至於即時性的話題，則另闢部落格 。網 址: http://doctorarts.blogspot.com/

 　

This website really reveal an ordinary person's major concern about our environment, no matter what the visible urban or rural landscape manifests, the webmaster may appreciate it's beauty and  criticize it's ugly part. And no matter what invisible culture or educational condition arises, the webmaster also  praises it's righteousness and traces it's wickedness as a Good Samaritan. Only the righteousness in culture or educational condition can bring the the beauty to our environment. As for the appreciation of beauty in urban or rural spaces, the webmaster depicts them using an animated gif method to express the inherent rhythm. We can enjoy the beauty of circulating in a city or nature slowly, carefully. Thus, animated gif can be seen as a motion painting that is absolutely different from traditional painting or photography.

This website has constructed for 10 years in old version, and the new version is 10th Anniversary.
